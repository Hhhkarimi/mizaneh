# Scientific Uncertainty Register

Prepared: 2026-07-30

| ID | Uncertainty | Affected goals | Product risk | Required treatment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| U-01 | Individual energy expenditure can differ materially from predictive equations | all | false precision and inappropriate target | show estimate range; calibrate only from multi-week trend; keep formula version |
| U-02 | Fixed activity multipliers depend on ambiguous self-report categories | all | systematic over/under-estimation | improve activity questions; compare with NASEM 2023 EER; expose uncertainty |
| U-03 | Static 7700 kcal/kg conversion ignores adaptation and tissue composition | weight loss | unrealistic timeline and blame | no guaranteed timeline; trend-based review |
| U-04 | One universal deficit percentage is not established | weight loss | excessive or ineffective restriction | treat 20% as legacy until approved; add safety and adequacy checks |
| U-05 | Sex-specific 1200/1500 floors are not a complete safety model | weight loss | unsafe plan may still pass | clinical exclusions, nutrient adequacy, size-aware policy |
| U-06 | Optimal hypertrophy surplus is not established | muscle gain | unnecessary fat gain or inadequate energy | conservative trial, bounded adjustment, no guarantee |
| U-07 | Rate of muscle gain varies with training age, genetics, sex, sleep, programme and adherence | muscle gain | unsupported promises | no pure-muscle or fixed-rate claims; monitor performance and waist/weight trend |
| U-08 | Healthy weight-gain evidence often comes from athletes or clinical malnutrition populations | weight gain | poor transfer to ordinary users | narrow automated scope; label product decisions; escalate clinical cases |
| U-09 | Meal-frequency evidence does not support one universal frequency | all | unnecessary rigidity | preference/appetite-based schedule; do not claim metabolic superiority |
| U-10 | Protein needs depend on training, age, energy balance and health status | all | over- or under-targeting | goal-specific range; contraindication screen; source version |
| U-11 | Current meal nutrition lacks carb, fat, micronutrients and structured ingredient weights | all | incorrect recalculation and replacement equivalence | do not claim complete nutrient equivalence until data model is upgraded |
| U-12 | Iranian food-composition data access/licensing is unresolved | all | legal/provenance risk | confirm licence before import; retain source and confidence fields |
| U-13 | Raw/cooked weights and recipe yields materially alter nutrient density | all | portion and recipe errors | mandatory preparation-state/yield fields for verified recipes |
| U-14 | Serious allergy safety cannot be guaranteed from five tags and free text | all | acute health risk | exclude complex/serious allergy cases; validate every post-edit action |
| U-15 | Weight measurements fluctuate with water, glycogen, food mass, menstrual cycle and sodium | all | overreaction to one reading | rolling trend and user-facing explanation |
| U-16 | Micronutrient completeness will be partial during data migration | all | false “adequate” labels | explicit missing-data state; never impute silently |
| U-17 | Supplements can be contaminated or contraindicated | muscle gain | health and anti-doping harm | separate optional module; professional review and quality warnings |
